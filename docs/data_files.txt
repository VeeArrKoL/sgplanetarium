Each of the included data files is described below:

aliens.csv: Describes the aliens on a planet with a given planetary seed and 4th coordinate.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 4th coordinate
* disposition: The aliens' disposition (see Disposition Lookup below)
* item: The alien's item (for trade for friendly aliens, or dropped by hostile aliens; see Alien Item Lookup below)
* price: The trade price for a friendly alien's item

animals.csv: Describes the animals on a planet with a given planetary seed and 3rd coordinate.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 3rd coordinate
* disposition: The animals' disposition (see Disposition Lookup below)
* complexity: The animals' complexity (see Complexity Lookup below)

hazards.csv: Describes the hazards present on a planet with a given planetary seed and 1st coordinate. Each hazard column contains a 1 if the hazard is present and 0 if it is not.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 1st coordinate
* toxic_atmosphere
* high_gravity
* irradiated
* magnetic_storms
* high_winds
* hot_solar_flare
* frigid_zones
* scary_noises
* nasty_gasses
* lewd_rock_formations

murderbots.csv: Describes the presence of murderbots on a planet with a given planetary seed and 6th coordinate.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 6th coordinate
* presence: The presence of murderbots (see Army Presence Lookup below)

plants.csv: Describes the plants on a planet with a given planetary seed and 2nd coordinate.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 2nd coordinate
* disposition: The plants' disposition (see Disposition Lookup below)
* complexity: The plants' complexity (see Complexity Lookup below)

quest.csv: Describes the available quest stage on a planet with a given planetary seed and 7th coordinate.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 7th coordinate
* type: The quest type available (see Quest Type Lookup below)
* step: The quest stage available

spants.csv: Describes the presence of spants on a planet with a given planetary seed and 5th coordinate.

Columns:
* pseed: The planetary seed
* letter: The 5th coordinate
* presence: The presence of spants (see Army Presence Lookup below)
